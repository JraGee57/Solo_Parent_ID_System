Requirement guide research — Virac, Catanduanes
=============================================

Reviewed on 12 October 2026. Applicant content is in `includes/requirement-guides.php`, selected by the configured `doc_key` through `RequirementGuideService`.

Scope and interpretation
------------------------

The guide explains how to request the documents already named in the database checklist. It does not change category eligibility, add documentary requirements, or promise approval. Local request steps that lack a published Virac procedure are explicitly labelled as suggested routes requiring office confirmation. National requirements do not establish a particular barangay's fees or turnaround time.

Verified local information
--------------------------

- [PSA Virac advisory, 31 March 2026](https://rsso05.psa.gov.ph/content/full-implementation-civil-registration-service-appointment-system-crs-outlet-virac): CRS appointments required from 6 April 2026, including National ID holders; use the actual requester's name. Appointment booking is free, distinct from document fees.
- [PSA Bicol directory](https://psa.gov.ph/directory/bicol-region): Catanduanes office at BL Jastrid Building, National Road, Cavinitan, Virac; listed contact 0920 968 1179. Office-directory information is not a guarantee of a particular service counter or opening hours.
- [Virac MCRO](https://virac-catanduanes.gov.ph/municipal-civil-registry-office/) and [Virac MSWDO](https://virac-catanduanes.gov.ph/municipal-social-welfare-development-office/): office references, not a verified current document-by-document service charter.
- [Virac civil-registration announcement](https://virac-catanduanes.gov.ph/free-issuance-of-civil-registry-certifications/): free local certifications were announced for 27 February 2026. This was a dated event; it must not be presented as an ongoing exemption or a free PSA-copy service.
- [PAO 2024 charter](https://pao.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PAO-Citizens-Charter-Handbook-2024-1st-Edition.final_.Rev_.4.pdf), directory p. 48: Virac District Office at DOJ Building, San Isidro Village; 0917 245 5236. It is an older directory, so applicants are told to confirm service availability and qualification. Do not promise universal free notarization.
- [NACC 2026 charter](https://www.nacc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NACC-Citizens-Charter-2026-2nd-Edition.pdf), directory p. 336: RACCO V at 2nd Floor, Tyler Building, Rosal Street, Barangay 35-Tinago, Legazpi City; 0917 191 6222; racco5@nacc.gov.ph. Contact before mainland travel. New legal processes and requests for copies of existing documents are different services.
- [DOH EBMC facility listing](https://ntp.doh.gov.ph/view-facility/?id=4331): San Isidro Village, Virac. This directory entry verifies a facility location, not a records-office procedure or incapacity-assessment service timeline.
- [BIR directory attachment, 2023](https://bir-cdn-preprod.e.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2085-2023%20Annex%202.pdf): RDO 69 in San Isidro Village. Use as an office lead, with current counter/service confirmation; no current local tax-document issuance promise.

Document-specific primary references
-----------------------------------

- PSA: [birth](https://psa.gov.ph/birth-certificate), [marriage](https://psa.gov.ph/marriage-certificate), [death](https://psa.gov.ph/death-certificate), [CENOMAR](https://psa.gov.ph/cenomar), [official online-channel directory](https://psa.gov.ph/civilregistration), and [requester/authorization guidance](https://rsso04a.psa.gov.ph/content/mga-alituntunin-sa-pag-isyu-ng-birth-certificate-death-certificate-marriage-certificate). Record identity details vary by document. Identity/authorization must follow the current requesting channel. Local civil-registry records are not automatically substitutes for required PSA copies.
- PSA National ID: [registration](https://philsys.gov.ph/registration-process/) and [supporting documents](https://philsys.gov.ph/supporting-documents/). National ID registration and CRS certificate requests are different services.
- Supreme Court: [traditional notarization identity/appearance discussion](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/17/63384), [2025 electronic-notarization rules](https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/24-10-14-sc-rules-on-electronic-notarization/), and [court locator](https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/court-locator/). In-person paper-affidavit instructions must not imply that ordinary video calls constitute regulated electronic notarization. Court-copy requests go to the issuing branch, which may be outside Virac.
- [Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act, PCW](https://pcw.gov.ph/republic-act-no-11861-expanded-solo-parents-welfare-act/): documentary context. Some pages blocked direct retrieval during research; indexed official passages were available. This source is not a Virac barangay procedure. Application checklist wording and MSWDO assessment govern which document is requested.
- [BI travel-record certification](https://immigration.gov.ph/services/travel-records-certification/): published form/checklist, payment and claim-stub process; location listed as BI Main Office. No Virac certification counter was verified. Travel movements alone do not establish employment.
- [DMW contract-copy rule](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/poea/agency/files/Licensing_2016_POEA%20Rules_Landbased.htm) and [DMW charter](https://dmw.gov.ph/resources/dmw/DMW-Citizens-Charter-1st-Edition-CY-2023.pdf): signed employment record route; MWO services depend on the country. A blank template is not proof of employment.
- [BJMP charter reference](https://r1.bjmp.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/03.26.2022_MC_ON_CITIZENS_CHARTER.pdf): existence of detention-certification service. This older regional publication is not a verified current Virac facility procedure. Ask the actual custodian, whether provincial jail, BJMP, or BuCor.
- [DOH hospital medical-record service example, 2026](https://fmogh.doh.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/FMOGH-CC-REMOVED-SIGNATORIES-1.pdf): demonstrates request and authorization controls. Its fees, counter and schedule are not applied to EBMC or another treating facility.

Maintenance
-----------

Recheck appointment rules, directory contacts and issuing-office instructions before changing the review date. Preserve distinct aliases for older checklist keys. Requirements added by administrators receive an explicit MSWDO-confirmation fallback rather than invented instructions. No private providers, unofficial payment links, exact local fees, or unverified release-time estimates were added.
